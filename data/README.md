## Regenerate
`python run_all.py --only data` rebuilds `processed/` and `outputs/tables/adf_results.csv` from the snapshot.
`--refresh-data` downloads a new dated snapshot (needs yfinance) and does not overwrite the old one.

## Processing steps (data stage, `src/quantedge/data/`)
1. Load snapshot → 2. keep dates where all 7 ETFs have a price (common sample starts 2004-11-18, GLD inception; missing-day counts in `outputs/tables/data_quality_missing.csv`) → 3. log returns (statistics) and simple returns (portfolio P&L) → 4. flag |z| > 5 (full-sample, audit only, nothing deleted) → 5. ADF per ETF (constant, AIC lag choice) → 6. FRED: DTB3 and DGS10 aligned to ETF trading days with past values only (carried forward at most 5 business days; 40 days needed it, see `outputs/tables/data_quality_fred_alignment.csv`), rf_daily = (1+DTB3)^(1/252)−1 → `processed/rates_daily.csv`; CPI with MoM/YoY inflation → `processed/cpi_monthly.csv` (use with a 1-month release lag).

7. Quality checks (`quality.py`, thresholds in `config/settings.yaml → data.quality`), flag only, nothing removed: stale prices (≥3 unchanged days), jumps (|r| > 6 × volatility from the previous 63 days only), spike-and-revert reversals, single-day losses below −10%, Inclán–Tiao variance break test. Outputs: `processed/data_quality_flags.csv`, `outputs/tables/data_quality_flag_counts.csv`, `outputs/tables/structural_breaks_inclan_tiao.csv`. Integrity errors (duplicate or unsorted dates, prices ≤ 0) stop the run.

8. Income: trailing-12-month distribution yield per ETF (`processed/distribution_yield_ttm.csv`). Cross-check: the annual gap between total-return and price-return growth matches the average yield to within 0.1 pp for every ETF (`outputs/tables/income_crosscheck.csv`).
